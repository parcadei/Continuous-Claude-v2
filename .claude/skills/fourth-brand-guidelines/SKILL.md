## Brand Essence

**Mission:** "Simplify and empower hospitality through intelligent technology"

**Brand Keywords:** Insightful · Human · Empowering · Reliable

**Four Principles:**
1. Clarity First — communicate simply and directly
2. Empower People — technology feels like a partner, not a barrier
3. Consistency Builds Trust — aligned visuals and message architecture
4. Human + Digital Harmony — data-driven insights with a warm, approachable tone

## Brand Architecture

### Fourth (Company Brand)
AI platform for hospitality operations. Established, trusted, professional. Character: experienced, innovative, optimistic, human.

### Fourth iQ (AI Technology Brand)
Predictive insights and intelligent automation. Smart, forward-thinking, empowering.

**CRITICAL iQ RULES:**
- ALWAYS: lowercase 'i', uppercase 'Q' → **"iQ"**
- NEVER: "IQ", "Iq", "iq"
- Standard tagline: **"Powered by iQ"** — NOT "uses iQ", "with iQ", "has iQ"

The iQ teal accent has become a defining visual across the Fourth brand.

### The Fourth Dimension
The extra dimension of intelligence beyond standard solutions. Four components: Beyond the Standard · AI-Powered Intelligence · Human-Centric · Holistic View.

- Capitalize both words: "Fourth Dimension"
- Always explain as "the extra dimension of intelligence"
- Connect to real business outcomes, never use without context
- Example: "Welcome to the Fourth Dimension — where your operation moves faster than time."

## Quick Reference: Color

| Color | Hex | Primary Use |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| Deep Blue | #0C4A7D | Primary brand, headers |
| Teal Green | #00B69F | CTAs, accents, AI/iQ signal — NOT body text |
| Sky Blue | #6fb4e3 | Supporting, backgrounds |
| Midnight Navy | #002747 | Text (as black alternative) — NOT backgrounds |
| Dark Gray | #373e42 | Body text |
| Soft White | #F5F5F5 | Subtle backgrounds |

WCAG 2.1: 4.5:1 minimum for body text. Teal on white = 2.4:1 — accent only.

Full specs → `references/colors.md`

## Quick Reference: Typography

Font: **Poppins** (all text). Body: 16pt Regular. Min: 14pt. Line height: 1.5x.
Headlines: Semibold 48/36/24/20pt. Emphasis: Medium or Semibold (not italic).

Full hierarchy → `references/typography.md`

## Quick Reference: Tone

**Four pillars:** Energetic · Insightful · Human · Confident

Tone mantra: *"We make intelligence feel effortless — and operations feel human again."*

Always: active voice, present tense for features, second person ("your team"), plain language.
Never: passive voice, jargon, sentences over 25 words, replacement language for AI.

Full pillar guidance with examples → `references/tone-voice.md`

## Workflows

### Creating New Content
1. Define objectives — goal, audience, desired action
2. Research and plan — gather data, outline key messages
3. Draft — Hook → Problem → Solution → Evidence → Action
4. Review — brand checklist, tone, simplify language
5. Final check — proofread, formatting, links, approval

### Reviewing Existing Content
1. Brand identity check — iQ capitalization, colors, fonts, logo
2. Tone analysis — four pillars, active voice, plain language
3. Message clarity — value prop, outcomes, evidence
4. Structure review — flow, scannable, strong CTA
5. Provide feedback — what works, specific issues, concrete improvements

Every piece of Fourth content must:
