# Fourth Messaging & Compliance

## Value Propositions

**Primary:** "Fourth delivers the Fourth Dimension — AI-powered intelligence that simplifies operations and empowers people in hospitality."

| Audience | Value Prop |
|----------|-----------|
| Operations Managers | Real-time visibility and proactive insights to optimize labor, reduce costs, improve efficiency |
| HR/People Leaders | Fair schedules, improved satisfaction, reduced turnover, better team tools |
| Executives | Strategic decisions with data, efficient scale, competitive advantage through AI |
| Frontline Staff | Fair schedules, clear communication, tools that make the job easier |

## Key Messages

**About Fourth:** Leading hospitality workforce management platform. Trusted by thousands of venues worldwide. Comprehensive integrated solution. Proven results and ROI.

**About Fourth iQ:** AI-powered predictive intelligence. Turns data into actionable insights instantly. Proactive, not reactive management. Human-centric — augments people, never replaces. Learns from patterns across thousands of venues.

**Never say:**
- "AI replaces managers" or any replacement language
- "Artificial intelligence" — use "AI-powered" or just "iQ"
- Technical jargon without explanation

## Proof Points

- "Reduce labor costs by 15%"
- "Improve staff retention by 20%"
- "Save 10+ hours per week on scheduling"
- "Trusted by 10,000+ hospitality venues"

Always use real customer names, quotes, and specific results achieved.

## Photography Guidelines

**Subject matter:** Real hospitality settings, diverse people (staff/managers/customers), natural interactions, technology in context.

**Quality:** 300 DPI print / 72 DPI web. Sharp focus, proper exposure, natural color balance.

**Types:**
- Staff interacting with software: real people in authentic settings, device in hand — can add "Powered by iQ" overlay
- Case studies: short depth of field, customer product/venue photography, lozenge shape with customer logo

**Avoid:** Generic stock photos, overly posed corporate shots, technology without people, low quality, inconsistent lighting.

## Brand Compliance Checklist

**Brand Identity:**
- [ ] Fourth iQ spelled correctly (lowercase i, uppercase Q)
- [ ] Colors match brand palette exactly
- [ ] Color usage meets accessibility rules
- [ ] Poppins font used
- [ ] Fourth logo used correctly (if applicable)

**Tone and Voice:**
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] Energetic, insightful, human, confident tone achieved
- [ ] Plain language (minimal jargon)
- [ ] Second person ("your," "you") when addressing customers

**Messaging:**
- [ ] "Powered by iQ" used correctly
- [ ] Fourth Dimension explained with context (if used)
- [ ] Focus on human empowerment, not replacement
- [ ] Specific outcomes and data included

**Content Quality:**
- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors
- [ ] Clear structure with headlines and sections
- [ ] Strong CTA included
- [ ] Scannable (bullets, short paragraphs, subheadings)

**Accessibility:**
- [ ] Color contrast meets WCAG 2.1 (4.5:1 body text)
- [ ] Text minimum 14pt (16pt recommended)
- [ ] Alt text for images

**Legal:**
- [ ] No unsubstantiated claims
- [ ] Customer quotes and data approved
- [ ] Copyright and attribution correct

## Common Mistakes

**Branding:** "Fourth IQ" (wrong caps), "iQ" alone, colors outside palette, non-Poppins fonts, Teal for body text.

**Writing:** Passive voice, jargon, sentences over 25 words, weak CTAs ("Click here"), corporate stiff tone.

**Tone:** Too corporate, too casual, overhyped claims ("Revolutionary game-changer!"), features without benefits.

**Messaging:** Features instead of benefits, no specific data, missing CTA, ignoring customer pain points.
