# Fourth Tone of Voice

Fourth's voice: experienced, trusted partner to hospitality operators — supercharged by intelligence. Complex technology made human, helpful, and empowering.

**Tone Mantra:** "We make intelligence feel effortless — and operations feel human again."

## The Four Pillars

### 1. Energetic
Active voice, strong action verbs, dynamic forward-moving language. Exciting without overhype.

- DO: "Transform your operations with real-time insights"
- DON'T: "Operations can be transformed with our solution"
- DO: "Drive efficiency across your entire operation"
- DON'T: "Our software helps with efficiency"

### 2. Insightful
Lead with data and evidence. Deep industry knowledge. Focus on outcomes and results. Educational and authoritative.

- DO: "Our analysis of 10,000+ hospitality venues shows..."
- DON'T: "We think that venues could benefit from..."
- DO: "Reduce labor costs by 15% while improving staff satisfaction"
- DON'T: "You might save money and your staff might be happier"

We help people understand why data matters and what to do next. Words turn data into meaning and meaning into action. Lead with outcomes, not outputs.

### 3. Human
Conversational, not corporate. Relatable and warm. Empathetic to customer challenges. Plain language, no jargon.

- DO: "We understand the complexity of managing shift schedules"
- DON'T: "Our workforce optimization algorithms facilitate resource allocation"
- DO: "Let's explore how this works for your team"
- DON'T: "One shall investigate the operational parameters"

We talk *with* customers, not *at* them. Show empathy for long hours, slim margins, relentless pressure.

### 4. Confident
Definitive statements. Own our expertise. Clear and direct. Confident without arrogance.

- DO: "This will improve your efficiency"
- DON'T: "This might potentially help with efficiency"
- DO: "Fourth iQ delivers actionable insights instantly"
- DON'T: "Fourth iQ may be able to provide some insights"

Confidence comes from proof, not hype. We don't overclaim; we demonstrate impact.

| Context | Tone Focus | Example |
|---------|------------|---------|

| Internal & Partner | Confident, unified, purposeful | "Together, we're leading hospitality into a new era of intelligent operations" |
